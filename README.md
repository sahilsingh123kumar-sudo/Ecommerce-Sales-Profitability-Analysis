# E-commerce Sales & Profitability Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes e-commerce sales and profitability using Microsoft Excel. The goal is to understand business performance across customer segments, product categories, regions, and years, and identify products generating losses.

## Objectives
- Analyze overall sales, profit, orders, and units sold.
- Compare customer segment performance.
- Evaluate profitability across regions and product categories.
- Examine yearly sales trends.
- Identify loss-making products.
- Present key findings through an Excel dashboard.

## Tools Used
- Microsoft Excel
- Excel formulas and KPI calculations
- Data cleaning and quality checks
- Data analysis and visualization
- Dashboard design

## Key Performance Indicators
| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | ₹22.97 lakh |
| Total Profit | ₹2.86 lakh |
| Profit Margin | 12.47% |
| Total Orders | 5,009 |
| Units Sold | 37,871 |
| Average Order Value | ₹458.56 |

## Key Findings
- **Technology** generated the highest profit among the three product categories.
- **Furniture** had a comparatively low profit margin of 2.49%, making it a priority for further investigation.
- **West** had the highest regional sales and profit, with a 14.94% profit margin.
- **Central** had the lowest regional profit margin at 7.92%.
- The analysis identified products with negative profit that may require further review.

## Data Quality
The dataset was checked for duplicate records, missing values, invalid dates, and numeric errors before analysis.

## Deliverables
- Excel workbook containing the data, analysis, and dashboard.
- Business insights and interview-preparation notes.

## Limitations
The analysis identifies patterns in the available data. It does not establish that discounts or any other single factor caused low profitability. Further investigation would be needed before making business decisions.
